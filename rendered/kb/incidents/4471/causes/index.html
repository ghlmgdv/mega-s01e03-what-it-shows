The two problems

parser — shared parser assumes month first

Day-first tenants send dates in day/month order, but the shared parser reads every date month-first. The day and month swap, affecting app, email and form reports. This is the larger group and needs its own engineering ticket: #1 · 379 reports.

hint — booking-v3 shows the wrong date order

For day-first tenants using booking-v3, the date field tells people to enter MM/DD/YYYY. People follow that hint, while the form interprets dates in the tenant's day-first order. This is a separate form defect, limited to booking-v3 form reports, and needs its own ticket. The desk tracks it as #2 · 33 reports.

Triage rule

Use the tenant's configured locale and booking form to assign cause.
